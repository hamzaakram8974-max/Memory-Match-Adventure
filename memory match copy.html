    How to play

    Turn over two cards per move and find every matching pair. A successful match keeps your streak alive for bonus points.

    Use a hint token to briefly reveal a pair. Match 10 pairs in a day to earn three hint tokens; unused hints stay saved. Match all pairs to earn up to three stars: fewer moves earn more stars.
